|---|---|---|
| reasons quoting words the report never said | 3/70 | 0/70 |
| "refused" in the ambiguous-failure item's reasons | 5/10 | 0/10 |
| verdict and record correct | 67/70 | 70/70 |

The same runs closed #220: the ambiguous failure was `failed_corrected` in 20/20, so the earlier
1-in-3 decline did not recur.

## 3. Findings

1. **The judge's conduct/interior boundary is unstable.** On the unchanged base prompt, conduct is
   misread as manufactured attribution 13–25% of the time, depending on the run and its provider
   mix. Per item, the same prompt swings widely: `j4-827-d` scored 10/20 in one run, and 6/20 in
   another.
2. **Interior assertions about another scope get admitted through an invented informant (#225).**
   On the base prompt, "payroll-ops only processes overtime claims filed before the 20th of the
   month" (`j4-833`) was admitted in **20/20** reps. Every admit went through the hearsay path with
   an informant the judge made up: the contributor's own role ("informant 'skill=eng-lead
   scope=s_fleet_dispatch'"), or the other scope named as its own informant. `j4-832` ("legal-review
   has a rule that…") was admitted in 2/20 the same way. `j4-834` ("apparently lab-safety has
   decided…") was declined 20/20.
3. **Prompt-text fixes on this boundary do not hold across provider mixes.** Unpinned measurements
   cannot gate wording. Further prompt work on this line is frozen until gates run on a
   provider-pinned judge (#224).

**Found and fixed before merge.** An earlier live run of the same build scored 0/10 on p5-02,
while every fake-judge test had passed.

1. The parse gate for acted_on verdicts ignored `acted_on_operator_item`. As a result, operator
   evidence was silently not written, and raised contributions were forced to decline at the issuer.
2. An out-of-vocabulary decision on the single-contribution path reached the database and returned a

eval gap was also closed: p5-01 now scores the issuer's own verdict.

**Also found and fixed in v1.16 (#221):** the relay cascade hard-coded `withdrawn` at every hop,
so a correction lost its identity one hop downstream. Relays now carry `claim_corrected` at every
hop.

## 7. P6: how the shipped judge condenses (baseline before part 2), 2026-09-26

**Build:** release/v1.16.0 @ `5e1a7e8` (P6 part 1 merged; no judge-input change).
**Judge:** `qwen/qwen3-235b-a22b-2507` via OpenRouter; the calls went to GMICloud 110, Novita 7,
Parasail 2 and Nebius 1.

**Setup.** Each over-budget scope holds one *examined* item and one *unexamined* item, and each item
carries three distinctive numbers (its fact markers). A new contribution forces the judge to condense
to `words(forcing) + 6`. **Differential** = the share of the examined item's markers that survived
minus the share of the unexamined item's markers that survived.

**The judge trims facts, not items.** It removes individual numbers from both items rather than
dropping either one whole, so drop rows (which are item-level) under-describe condensation (#227).

| variant | examined item | mean differential | SD | n |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1 | corroborated (a held outcome) | −0.067 | 0.137 | 20 |
| v3 | **correcting** (the content of a failed outcome) | **−0.200** | 0.381 | 20 |
| pooled (v1, v2, v3) | — | −0.089 (95% CI −0.156 to −0.033) | 0.245 | 60 |

v2 was degenerate: every rep kept all numbers, so the variant is being replaced before the gate.

**Finding, stated before part 2 is measured:** on the shipped judge, examined facts survive
condensation slightly *less* often than unexamined ones. The judge sheds a **correction's** facts
first (−0.20). That is the echo risk the philosopher named: once the correcting content is condensed
away, a re-contributed retracted claim has nothing to be declined against. Part 2 exists to change
this. If it does not, this goes in the README known limits with the number.

## 8. P6 part 2 (examined context before condensation): gate, 2026-09-26

**Builds.** Before = release/v1.16.0 @ `977ba4c`. After = the merged tree `0f33e36` (feat/v16-p6-examined-context @ `d4cf404`).
**Method.** The two builds were interleaved in four 10-rep chunks, giving 120 reps per side.
**Judge.** `qwen/qwen3-235b-a22b-2507` via OpenRouter. The before side was served by GMICloud 238 and Novita 1; the after side by GMICloud 179 and Novita 61.

| variant (examined item) | before: mean (SD) | after: mean (SD) |
|---|---|---|
| v1: corroborated | −0.033 (0.101) | +0.017 (0.130) |
| v2 | −0.125 (0.233) | +0.319 (0.294) |
| v3: **correcting** | −0.200 (0.391) | **+0.692** (0.423) |
| **pooled (n = 120)** | **−0.119** (0.276) | **+0.342** (0.411) |

**Result.** After − before = **+0.462**, with a bootstrap 95% CI of [0.377, 0.552]. The pre-set floor was
0.15, so the gate passes. The shipped build's echo risk (a correction's facts shed first) is
reversed: corrections now survive condensation.

**Limits.**
- A lone corroborated item (v1) barely moves.
- The provider mixes differed between the two sides, because routing follows the prompt (#224).
  An earlier all-Novita baseline was −0.07, and the effect is too large to be explained by the
  provider mix.
- Scopes with no examined items get the unchanged input, pinned by the golden corpus.
