# Evidence: a child's own directive under an inherited one (#237, v1.17, 2026-10-04)

**What changed.** When an ordinary judgment declines a contribution, and its reasoning names an inherited (ancestor) directive as the conflict, the engine asks the judge once more with a separate tool. The second call asks for the relation: `contradicts`, `exempts`, `refines` or `tightens`. The engine then checks that answer against the contribution's text and the inherited directive's text.
- A verified `refines` or `tightens` reinstates the judge's original classification: a decision returns as a directive, an observation as context. The contributor is bound to its own scope.
- `contradicts`, `exempts`, an unreadable answer, a failed check, or any other open decline ground leaves the first decline standing.

**The checks** follow the philosopher's rulings (2026-10-03):
- Every guard reads the whole contribution, never only the quoted span: exemption language, quantifier softening ("usually", "most"), polarity and change markers, and claims that the inherited rule is outdated.
- A contribution whose subject shares a word with the inherited rule's subject is held to the tightening test.
- A tightening keeps every value of the parent or replaces it with a stricter one, in the direction the parent's own wording sets.
- A universal scope phrase in the parent ("every day", "always") must survive.
- A tightening limited to part of the child's scope ("in house 3, …") is still a tightening.

**How it was measured.** The strata-evals file bridge, with Claude (Sonnet) answering each judge request in turn. These numbers are directional, and not comparable with pinned-qwen numbers. ## Stated limits

- **A refinement whose subject shares a word with the inherited rule's subject is held to the tightening test.** "Chilled pallets … 0–4 °C" under "Frozen pallets … at or below -18 °C" stays declined.
- **A tightening that also says the inherited rule is outdated stays declined.** The reason asks the contributor to resubmit the stricter rule alone, because a child may tighten an inherited rule but not retire it.
- **Word-list proxies.** The subject and scope-phrase checks are mechanical proxies, and a comparator applies only where the parent's value sits in a recognised comparative pattern. Elsewhere the judge's relation answer stands, recorded.
- **Single-contribution path only.** Batched judgments aren't covered.
