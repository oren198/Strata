## P3: Before rev 3: why 23/47

On rev 1–2 the disposition was a separate `outcome_disposition` tool field. qwen reached the right verdict ("Held: … confirms the claim") but never filled that field, on the first call or on the re-ask, so fail-closed declined it. This is the known pattern (#209's changed_circumstance, M1's attestation fields). Ruling c′ had anticipated it: the decision is the one field this judge always fills. Rev 3 made the four values the `decision` enum itself, on `acted_on` calls only, and the score went from 23/47 to 44/47.

### Found and fixed before the release

1. **A correction of an ancestor's item was reported as applied when nothing changed.** P3 recorded `claim_corrected` although the claim was held elsewhere; P2 then read it as replaced. Fixed: the owner is told as an input change, and P2's reading is three-way and derived live (held / replaced / corrected, owner not yet acted).
2. **The judge-call shape broke externally supplied judges.** P3 passed a new keyword to every `judge()` call, and every out-of-repo judge got a 500 on each `/contribute`. Fixed: the keyword is passed only when set, pinned with a judge that has the pre-P3 signature. The strata-evals suite is now a release step.
3. **The judge never withdrew a corrected published claim**, so readers were never told. Fixed: the engine withdraws the owner's own published items that carry the corrected claim verbatim, and the record distinguishes the engine's act from the judge's.
4. **An over-broad first design** notified every child and peer about claims most of them never received. It was narrowed before any code was written.
