# Evidence: a child may tighten an inherited rule, not change it (1.17.1, 2026-10-05)

**What changed.** After judgment, a directive admitted from a session bound to the judged scope is compared with each inherited directive (ancestor and operator) on the same subject. One that loosens the inherited rule, exempts from it, softens its "all", narrows when or where it applies, flips its polarity or calls it outdated is admitted as context with an engine note instead. The check makes no judge call and doesn't change what the judge is sent; a test pins the inputs byte-identical.

**How it was measured.** No judge key was spent. The release tree is `feat/v1171-admit-side-inherited-check` at `75bbdd1`. Bridge numbers come from the strata-evals file bridge, with Claude answering each judge request: they are directional, not pinned.

## Stated limits

- "Same subject" is a leading-noun-phrase match. A refinement whose subject shares a word with the inherited rule's subject (chilled versus frozen pallets) is held to the tightening test and becomes context.
- A tightening the check can't read as stricter becomes context: a spelled-out number, or a range.
- A contradiction phrased without a value, polarity word or marker gives the check nothing to compare, so it still rests on the judge.
- In a batch, the judge's own context rewrite is kept and the held lines are appended.
