# Evidence: false holds fixed in the inherited-directive check (1.17.2, 2026-10-06)

**What was wrong.** 1.17.1's check held some legitimate child directives as context, so they lost their binding force. A new held-out set showed four cases:
- a tightening under an upper bound, read in the wrong direction ("a maximum of 7 days" under "a maximum of 14 days"; "never above −80 °C" under "never above −70 °C");
- an added own clause read as a polarity flip ("off at or before 19:30 and the nozzles locked" under "off at or before 20:00");
- a rule about a different thing sharing a place word read as the same subject ("the fuel dock spill kit" under "fuel dock pumps").

The failures were safe: nothing contradicting was admitted. But the legitimate rules lost their force.

**How it was measured.** No judge key was spent. The release tree is `fix/v1172-inherited-false-holds` at `8a170ba`. The held-out set is strata-evals `j1_drift`, 48 items labelled blind by the philosopher, covering a grandparent rule, a child's exception and a grandchild's adoption; it was built for #244. "Replay" means the Claude answers recorded on the file bridge against 1.17.1 were fed through 1.17.2. Judge inputs are unchanged by this patch: the requests were re-rendered and compared byte for byte on `j1_drift` and `j1_refinement`, with only ids and timestamps differing on the child-scope set. So the same judge decisions meet the new check.

- The check alone still misses 12 of the 18 directive-form exceptions in `j1_drift`: all 6 under the weak (value-free) rules, and 6 reworded or plain adoptions under strong rules. On this set the judge declined all of them. Raising the check's own catch rate is v1.18 (#244).
